I'm a post-doc in the research unit Cyclic Optimization (Cyclop) at Leipzig University. My research interests include:
  • Phonological Typology
  • Papuan Languages
  • Nominal Syntax
  • Phonology-Morphology Interface
  • Pitch Accent
